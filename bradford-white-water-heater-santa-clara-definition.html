Replacement vs. Repair

Repair retains the existing water heater while addressing a repairable problem. Replacement removes or retires the existing unit and introduces another system. Property and equipment conditions influence which path is appropriate.

Storage-Tank vs. Tankless

A storage-tank water heater stores heated water for use, while tankless equipment heats water in response to demand. Their infrastructure and operating requirements can differ, so neither should be assumed universally preferable.

Gas vs. Electric

Gas and electric water heaters depend on different energy infrastructure. Changing energy source can introduce additional property-specific requirements.

Heat-Pump vs. Conventional Electric

Heat-pump water heaters use a different heating approach than conventional electric resistance equipment and may involve different space, electrical, condensate, and installation considerations.
